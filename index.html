Music Style

If you supply the files, you can play whatever god forsaken devil music you like. But if you leave it up to me you will be blessed in the glorious divine light of Dub.

Inspired by the heavenly rhythms forged in the terror and civil unrest of late 1960's, early 1970's Jamaica. Cobbled into existence with scrap electronics and a deep connection to god and country. Dub is the mischievous and rebellious child of Dancehall, Ska, and Reggae.

Dub music soothes the savage beast. It shines light in the darkness of our suffering. It invites all in our great family to FEEL the heartbeat of the earth under our feet.
